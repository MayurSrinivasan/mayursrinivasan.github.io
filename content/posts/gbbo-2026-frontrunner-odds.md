Watching Tom Whittaker cruise through *The Great British Bake Off* Series 17, it is tempting to assume the trophy is already his. Entering the semi-finals after Episode 8, he holds three Star Baker titles, multiple technical wins, and a Hollywood handshake.

Even when you combine every signal—Star Bakers, technical rankings, and handshakes—Tom's semi-final odds are **not** the highest in *Bake Off* history.

{{< gbbo-comparison >}}

Two past frontrunners entered the semi-finals with an even stronger statistical grip on the tent:

- **Jasmine Mitchell (Series 16, 2025)** entered Episode 9 with **4 Star Baker wins**—including the Episode 8 quarter-final—and went on to win both the semi-final and the series.
- **Richard Burr (Series 5, 2014)** also entered Episode 9 with **4 Star Baker wins** (including back-to-back wins in Episodes 7 and 8), picked up a fifth in the semi-final, and still lost in the finale.{{< sidenote >}}Steph Blackwell (Series 10) is another cautionary tale: she entered the finale with 4 Star Bakers and lost to David Atherton, who won the entire series with zero.{{< /sidenote >}}

Series 17 is also more balanced than it looks at first glance. Every remaining semi-finalist has won Star Baker at least once. Moyin enters Episode 9 with momentum from winning the quarter-final and holds the season's first handshake. Mo also has a handshake, and Gabe has a technical win.

Handshakes and technical wins do not guarantee a finale victory either. In Series 15, Dylan Bachelet racked up three technical wins and three handshakes without winning the crown.

Tom is the clear statistical favorite heading into the semi-finals. But because *Bake Off* resets the scoreboard every week, a single shaky Showstopper can still flip the odds.

Regardless of who wins, Series 17 has already hit a milestone: after Connie's quarter-final exit, it is the first *Bake Off* season with no women in the semi-finals.{{< sidenote >}}Series 3 (2012) and Series 14 (2023) both had all-male finals, but at least one woman reached the semi-finals in each.{{< /sidenote >}}
